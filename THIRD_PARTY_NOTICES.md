# Third-party notices

GoLean's own files are under the Apache License 2.0 (`LICENSE`), copyright Oath Technologies,
2026. The tree also carries material copied or derived from other projects, each under its own
license. This file is the inventory: what the material is, where it sits, which upstream revision
it comes from, what license governs it, and where the notice that license asks for lives. It is a
living document (written 2026-10-26, [AGENT], lane `records/dc-packaging-1026`; the deep-clean
review's D1/DC05); `scripts/check-notices`, a `scripts/ci` speedbump, checks that the two notice
files named here exist and that no tracked file outside the inventoried subtrees carries an
upstream copyright header. The gitignored reference checkouts under `deps/` are not part of the
tree and carry their own license files.

## Where the layout comes from

The notice for a copied subtree sits at the root of that subtree, as a vendored tree carries its
own `LICENSE` file (Go's `vendor/` and the usual `third_party/` layouts; license scanners look
for `LICENSE*` there), and this root-level file is the single inventory that maps every piece of
third-party material to its notice. The Apache `NOTICE` mechanism is not used for this: under
Apache-2.0 §4(d) a `NOTICE` file carries the attribution notices of the Work itself, and GoLean
has none to give; keeping the third-party inventory in a separately named file avoids
conflating the two.
