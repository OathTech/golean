## Dimension summary
The arc-4 tip is technically sound where it claims to be proved (the four R-form round lemmas, `round_induction`, the S1 leaf and the etcd discharge are all real, kernel-backed, hatch-free), and most individual gaps are disclosed somewhere in a docstring or log. But the DIRECTION does not converge the way the summary layer claims. Three structural problems: (1) the U26 headline mechanism, `round_induction`, contains no coupling whatsoever between the concrete round chain and the abstract net chain — its safety corollaries follow from the `habs` premise alone, so the "simulation induction / refinement mapping" it advertises (and the layer-C §3 carry it says it mechanizes) is not what landed; the seeded corollary's docstring further asserts "nothing left premised on the abstract side", which is false of its own `habs` argument. (2) The T1 open-obligation census O1–O7 — the document that is supposed to say what stands between this tree and `AgreementT1` — omits whole obligation classes: `violations = 0` covers twelve `t.viol` sites including six harness-liveness/error guards, and the entire S2/S3 assembly (the separate `HNet` carrier, `HistInv`, an `S23CheckerInterface` instance, the S3 anomaly sub-check) is not a named item. ## Dimension summary
The technical layer is in better shape than the volume suggests: `round_induction` is a genuine, correctly-proved simulation induction (I checked it hypothesis-by-hypothesis — the `ih` is applied at `l.canon'` under `ChainedFrom`, the placement `r, na₀` really is preserved, `FamTrace.cons` really carries per-boundary `FrameSim`, and `RoundLemmaShape.refl`/`stepFnIter_sim` are sound); the R-form census numbers for RoundMar check out arithmetically (5738+5816+7500+7170 = 26224, draws 7+1+0+2 = |πMar| = 10, with stream residuals matching link-by-link) and its kernel pins do tie to the pinned lowering via `bfTB = twinLowered.{funcs,methods,methodSets}` plus the `Expr.eqbF` shape pins; `SeedChoiceInvariance` is a `def : Prop` with exactly one consumer, taken as a hypothesis, and the `~ₘ` mask's justification chain (sole reader `pastElectionTimeout` → sole caller `tickElection` → `RawNode.Tick`, never called by the driver) is verifiable end-to-end in the lowered subject; the new `check-golden [twin-wire]` block closes the frontend↔bytes half of the wire pin. The defects are concentrated in two places. First, the abstract side of the induction is unconstrained data: `RoundLink.N/N'` are never tied to `absTwinRead`, so `FamTrace.reach`/`safety` say nothing about the Go program, yet the module header, `RoundStatement`'s "carried relation" paragraph and the lane log all assert the paired ("absState trace = specRound trace") form that the same module elsewhere disclaims. Second, one real code-level fail-open: `collectFix`'s stability test compares view-list lengths while `bumpView` widens entries in place, so the `~` carrier's reachability fixpoint can stop with an untraversed window and drop content without a flag. Supporting findings cover the corpus split's silent weakening of the non-vacuity gate for a LIVE law, a round-boundary reader that is not fail-closed as documented, and three doc-vs-code drifts.
