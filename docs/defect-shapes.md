# Defect shapes — the record of `docs/BUGS.md` classified for the neighbourhood sweep

A living document of the defect-neighbourhood campaign
(`docs/2026-10-27_defect-neighbourhood-campaign.md`, approved [USER]
2026-10-27): step 1's deliverable (§2–§3) and step 2's handoff (§4). One row
per entry of `docs/BUGS.md` — the 133 on main at `aa49ebcf`, the three pending
on `stage/train-20-1026` (BUG-134–136, marked «stage»), and the 23 late
entries step 1 added (BUG-137–159). Written 2026-10-27 by the [AGENT] lane
`records/defect-shapes-1027`; the sweep lanes fill the last column and update
the counts in §3 as step 2 lands; dated and frozen at the release.

How the classification was made: each entry was read and its shape named in
a few words from its own text and its site; the names were then normalized
into the vocabulary of §1. The plan's §3 seed vocabulary was the starting
point; the reading moved it: «representation leaks into identity» and «a
value crosses a conversion without its effect» stay (S4, S2) but the identity
KEYS the wire mints are a shape of their own (S3), «evaluation order» split
into three shapes with different remedies (S6 the assignment phases, S7
spec-ordered events the hoist reorders, S8 spec-unsequenced pairs realized as
one member) plus evaluation COUNT (S9), «zero values, nil-ness and aliasing»
split between nil TYPING at a sink (S1) and nil-ness/identity (S4) and binding
(S13), the frontend's lowering split between coverage holes (S17) and the
admission boundary (S20), and the apparatus between the readout (S22), the
tool boundaries (S24) and the resource budgets (S23).

## 3. The step-2 order and each shape's matrix axes (the handoff)

Order (plan §4): shapes whose found instances were wrong answers with status
ok come first — the plan names identity, conversions, absorbing defaults,
admission and panics — then the other wrong-answer shapes, then the
wrongly-stuck and refusal shapes, then the readout. Within a shape the most
recent instance first (the member lists below are in that order). Each
shape's line names the matrix the sweep lane enumerates BEFORE probing
(plan §4 step 1: the same shape at the other types, the other routes, the
other sites); the lane writes the matrix into its entry's `- Neighbourhood:`
line and this table's `lane` column, and updates the counts above.
